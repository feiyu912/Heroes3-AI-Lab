# Native engineering qualification for scripted supply

Use only a reviewed new supply capsule after a locked build and exact accepted-V8 restoration. The dedicated fixture has two actual owned heroes, finite initial town army and an inert EmptyAI opponent. It is a mechanism fixture, not a complete strategic game or ability score.

Required evidence: actual loaded roster/roles and town stock, successful native movements by both actors, persistent roles/job across two own turns, real town-to-courier pickup, real hero meeting/query, compatible whole-stack courier-to-main delivery with a reserve army, exact conservation and native request/ACK bindings, query closure and continued scheduling. EndTurn notification/ACK and ExchangeDialog/movement ordering must be observed rather than assumed. A controlled partial stop is not a normal game, win/loss, resumable boundary or learning sample.

No teacher/broker/neural weights/optimizer are used. Old schema4 and battle rules remain frozen. Native control restore is explicitly unavailable in this prototype. Failures retain raw evidence and lead to a new isolated revision; no fabricated query resolution or ACK and no relaxing legacy auditors to certify these different scripted semantics.
