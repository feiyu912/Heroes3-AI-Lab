But the **first different real action is request17 on day3**, while the **first release is request155 at trace756, after original-hero disappearance trace683/day15**. Therefore neither its early death nor the full negative paired score can be directly attributed to the release branch's target erasures.

Source/input freeze passed, yet the opponent/world chronology differs before the intervention becomes active.

The added exact ArmyDescriptor(hero,true) source reads own stack descriptors and sums public unit AI value/count (InfoAboutArmy.cpp20–51); it does not itself request RNG or modify game state. This read-only review has not established whether opponent scheduling, initial/client AI randomness, timing/cache effects or another engine factor explains the early difference. Do not turn that unresolved cause into a fabricated deterministic counterfactual or claim all opponent trajectories are fixed by server seed. Detailed raw request/log SHA and early NPC prefix are preserved in BINDING_AND_CAUSAL_LIMIT.json.

Phase A consequently stays unapproved as a default: no observed benefit, very few newly selected goals, and a material causal/reproducibility caveat on the sole score discordance. Do not add PPO rounds or tune thresholds on this pair to call it improved. The next typed EndTurn interface can be developed from preserved baseline e198/server-ready-v8 with its own mechanics protocol; it must not silently include this failed/unproven pressure-release intervention.
